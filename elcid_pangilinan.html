Elcid C. Pangilinan is a seasoned development executive with decades of distinguished service at the Land Bank of the Philippines, where he currently holds the position of Senior Vice President. His career is defined by a commitment to national development, financial inclusion, and the empowerment of communities through accessible and strategic banking.

Beyond his executive role, Elcid is the Chair of the Chevening Alumni Foundation of the Philippines — a testament to his dedication to nurturing the next generation of leaders and fostering ties between Filipino professionals and the global community shaped by the UK's prestigious Chevening Scholarship programme.
