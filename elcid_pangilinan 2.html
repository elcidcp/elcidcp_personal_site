Elcid C. Pangilinan is a seasoned development executive and public finance leader with over three decades of distinguished service spanning banking, international development, policy advisory, and academia. He currently serves as Senior Vice President at the Land Bank of the Philippines, the country's premier development financial institution.

His expertise spans strategy formulation and planning, capacity development, monitoring and evaluation (M&E), management of Official Development Assistance (ODA), and ICT for development. He has worked with world-class institutions including the United Nations Development Programme, USAID, and the Millennium Challenge Account – Philippines.

A proud Chevening Scholar and Hubert H. Humphrey Fellow, Elcid brings a global perspective to every initiative — anchored always in the goal of inclusive and sustainable national development.
